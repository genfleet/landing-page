# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Genfleet primarily serves company owners, CEOs, founders, and other business decision-makers evaluating how their organization will deploy and operate AI agents. Developers are an important secondary audience who need to see credible support for their existing frameworks and workflows. The page must make the business value understandable without requiring technical expertise while retaining enough specificity to earn technical trust.

## Product Purpose

Genfleet is the managed cloud platform for deploying, operating, and scaling AI agents built with different frameworks. The landing page should establish Genfleet as the dependable infrastructure layer that gives organizations one place to manage their agents, move them into production, and grow their usage with confidence.

## Brand Personality

Confident, operational, and composed. Genfleet should feel like enterprise-grade infrastructure without becoming cold, bureaucratic, or overloaded with technical language.

## Anti-references

- Developer-only messaging that excludes nontechnical decision-makers.
- Loud startup gradients, oversized accent-color fields, or brightly colored CTA backgrounds.
- Playful or trendy typography that weakens operational credibility.
- Generic AI claims, abstract futurism, and vague promises unsupported by concrete product capabilities.
- Decorative hover effects or motion that makes the interface feel restless.

## Design Principles

1. Lead with the organizational outcome, then substantiate it with technical compatibility.
2. Make infrastructure feel clear and controlled rather than complex or intimidating.
3. Use concise, declarative language that communicates authority without hype.
4. Demonstrate how Genfleet connects existing agents to dependable cloud operations.
5. Reserve the brand accent for navigation, status, and small moments of emphasis.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.2 AA contrast and interaction standards. Preserve visible keyboard focus, semantic structure, reduced-motion support, and readable type across mobile and desktop. Do not rely on color alone to communicate state.
